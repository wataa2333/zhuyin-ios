Lexicon.tsv is a derived dictionary. The Rime Ice phrase data is selected by weight,
converted from Pinyin to Zhuyin, annotated with tones, and deduplicated. The source
files below and Tools/build_lexicon.py reproduce the bundled dictionary.

- iDvel/rime-ice, cn_dicts/base.dict.yaml (phrase entries), GPL-3.0-only.
  https://github.com/iDvel/rime-ice/blob/99662ba01e3affa6a0f009174ca530b887bac3b6/cn_dicts/base.dict.yaml
- mozillazg/phrase-pinyin-data, pinyin.txt (phrase tone readings), MIT.
  https://github.com/mozillazg/phrase-pinyin-data/blob/4afa3226ce53c9138b939e83c3bb294dbe0da511/pinyin.txt
- Rime pinyin_simp.dict.yaml (single characters), derived from Android Pinyin IME, Apache-2.0.
  https://github.com/rime/rime-pinyin-simp/tree/0c6861ef7420ee780270ca6d993d18d4101049d0
- mozillazg/pinyin-data, MIT.
  https://github.com/mozillazg/pinyin-data/tree/fe29ad32bdf352b31a02b9936e537f575432e9a6
- OpenCC TSCharacters.txt, Apache-2.0.
  https://github.com/BYVoid/OpenCC/tree/fae2a0a911c30764e669a7d8b0591cd8e852934a
