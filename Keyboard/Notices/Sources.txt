Lexicon.sqlite is a derived, preindexed dictionary. The Rime Ice phrase tables are
merged without a phrase-count cutoff, converted from Pinyin to Zhuyin, annotated
with tones, and deduplicated. Unknown or ambiguous inferred tones use tone zero.
Lexicon.tsv is the smaller legacy fallback. Tools/build_lexicon.py and the fixed
source files below reproduce the SQLite dictionary.

- iDvel/rime-ice, cn_dicts/base.dict.yaml (phrase entries), GPL-3.0-only.
  https://github.com/iDvel/rime-ice/blob/99662ba01e3affa6a0f009174ca530b887bac3b6/cn_dicts/base.dict.yaml
- iDvel/rime-ice, cn_dicts/ext.dict.yaml, cn_dicts/tencent.dict.yaml, cn_dicts/others.dict.yaml, GPL-3.0-only.
  https://github.com/iDvel/rime-ice/tree/99662ba01e3affa6a0f009174ca530b887bac3b6/cn_dicts
